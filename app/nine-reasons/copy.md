# An Australian Funeral Director’s 9 Reasons Family Safekeep Works When The Notes And Folders You Already Have Don’t

**Sienna Cunningham✓**

August 29, 2026 · 6 min read · Funeral Director, 23 years

**Summary:** After twenty-three years sitting with families during the first weeks after a death, I have learned that most people are not completely unprepared. There is usually a will. There are usually folders, passwords and notes somewhere. What is missing is the practical map of the life underneath them. These nine reasons show how Family Safekeep gives families that map for $27.

## 1. It starts where the family actually starts

Families do not begin with the will. They begin with the phone, because the bank wants a code, the email is on it, the photographs are on it and half the rest of the household eventually leads back to it.

Most planners begin with final wishes or bank details. Family Safekeep starts with the practical information somebody else would need first, because that is the order a family actually lives.

## 2. It records what comes after the password

I have watched enough families try to get into accounts to know that the password is often only the first step. They type it correctly, and the bank sends a code to a phone nobody can unlock, or the email asks for a recovery address nobody knew existed.

A password list can look complete until somebody actually needs to use it. **Family Safekeep records the route into the account, not just the password beside it.**

## 3. It tells them what exists before they have to go looking for it

Years ago, a month of post could reveal most of a household. Bank statements arrived. The super fund wrote. Insurance renewed. Bills landed on the mat.

Now almost all of that happens quietly online. A family may not even know which accounts, policies or subscriptions exist, never mind where to find them.

Family Safekeep creates the inventory first, so the person dealing with everything knows what they are looking for.

## 4. It covers the part the will leaves to the family

I work alongside solicitors regularly and they are good at what they do. The will gets handled. The legal paperwork moves. Then the family is left with everything the law does not organise for them: the super fund, the direct debits, the utilities, the insurance, the subscriptions, the accounts and the practical information that keeps the household running.

That is the part families end up piecing together themselves. **Family Safekeep is for that part.**

## 5. It is built around an Australian household

An Australian family has its own practical landscape: superannuation, council rates, banks and credit unions, mortgages, insurers, utilities, vehicle information, medical contacts and the ordinary administration that keeps life running.

A planner only helps if the questions make sense to the person filling it in. Family Safekeep is designed around the information an Australian household is actually likely to need.

## 6. It does the thinking before you start filling it in

The problem with a blank spreadsheet is not that it is difficult to type into. The problem is deciding what belongs there.

You have to work out the categories, the questions, what somebody else would need and what you may have forgotten. That is why so many homemade systems remain half finished.

Family Safekeep has already done that thinking. **You answer the questions instead of designing the system.**

## 7. It is written for the person who has to use it after you

Your own notes make sense because you wrote them. “Insurance folder.” “House account.” “Ask Sarah.” You know what each one means.

Somebody else may not.

Family Safekeep is organised so another person can understand what exists, where it is and what they may need to do next, without already knowing the household.

## 8. You have thirty days to decide it is not for you

Let me be straightforward, because you may have bought a planner before and it is sitting in a drawer right now.

Take thirty days with Family Safekeep. Open it, work through the first section, the one about the phone, and see whether the questions are the ones your family would actually need answered.

If it is not the one, ask for a refund. **Every cent is refunded, and there is nothing to post back or pay to return.**

That is a fair way to find out whether this is the planner you will finally fill in.

## 9. The families who arrive with one completed are out in weeks, not months

I can tell within ten minutes of meeting a family whether somebody wrote things down.

The ones who did are through the practical work in a couple of weeks, and they spend the rest of the time doing what they should be doing: being with each other, remembering the person they have lost, and taking the time they need.

The ones who did not are still making phone calls months later, trying to find out what exists, who to contact and where the answers were kept.

When a family comes through my door with Family Safekeep completed, **the difference is not subtle.** They have somewhere to start, instead of another list of questions nobody can answer.

When the practical information has been properly handed over, the difference is simple.

They know what exists.

They know where to look.

And they know where to start.

That is what Family Safekeep is for.
